## Acceptance standard

A customer-ready result needs no material corrective pass before delivery:

- Identity, contact details, geographic scope, services, credentials, quotations, and transactional claims agree with their retained authority. Source material is used accurately and in context.
- The homepage displays the exact supported Google aggregate rating and its supplied reviews URL together. No invented rating, URL, or individual third-party review prose. Browser research remains the authorized enrichment path; no paid Places API.
- The route set is proportionate to the business and its useful source material. Every retained route has a clear customer purpose, substantive relevant content, accurate metadata, and reachable navigation.
- Copy speaks directly to customers, uses concrete business evidence, and answers route-specific questions without internal research language or generic filler.
- Design is coherent across the site and suited to the business. Composition follows the content and customer action; shared shells are acceptable. Real imagery has appropriate resolution, role, caption, and crop.
- Desktop, tablet, phone, opened navigation, and form states work and look intentional. Objective safety, accessibility, factual, capability, and functional checks pass.
- A normal owner edit preserves unrelated source and behavior. Publishing and managed form delivery work through the released hosted system.

Subjective judgments remain advisory to product authoring. Review may reject an experiment for delivery quality without converting its taste findings into a hard release rule. Optional taste alternatives do not prevent acceptance, but missing evidence, weak primary copy, visibly unfinished composition, or a defective route does.
