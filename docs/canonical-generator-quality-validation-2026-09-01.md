# Canonical generator quality validation — September 1, 2026

## Decision

**Superseded readiness claim:** The canonical generator remains the recommended go-forward architecture, but the two cited artifacts are rejected and do not support product promotion. A later complete audit found a severe below-fold layout failure in Wallcovering R2 and authority, destination, legal-content, and location-scope defects in Automotive R2. The architecture is cleaner; its production readiness is unproven until corrected fresh runs pass.

This decision keeps one system:

- broad model authorship in editable source;
- compact contextual guidance for design and copy judgment;
- trusted runtime ownership only for managed behavior;
- immutable owner/source authority;
- mechanical inspection feedback during authoring; and
- deterministic safety, fact, capability, accessibility, and functional verification at finish.

No deterministic page composer, visual template engine, automatic critic, repair orchestration, or aesthetic release gate was added.

- canonical final verification now browser-checks every approved live route at desktop and phone sizes; the model-facing inspection remains a bounded four-route feedback tool; and

The detector was calibrated against both the rejected address strip and the accepted R2 resource callout. It measures actual rendered line fragments, so R2's readable 159px editorial columns retain an advisory without being mislabeled as functionally unreadable.

The accepted wallcovering source contains none of those phrases. Whole-site advisory evidence now reports route depth, copy similarity, thin routes, image use, metadata, reachability, raw-data language, and internal research language across the complete inventory.

The corrected human review rejects both artifacts. Numeric Website Health diagnostics remain non-authoritative and cannot erase an exact factual or functional failure. Full evidence is retained with the corresponding private design-review artifacts.

## Upstream corrections validated

- Real service routes become offering authority; service-area/location routes, blog articles, Schema.org categories, and generic calls to action do not.
- A root-level service route remains eligible even when the source page classifier labels it generically.
- Desktop semantic navigation and the managed phone disclosure are authored as separate visible presentations, avoiding the earlier hidden-desktop-navigation failure.
- Route-local visual inspection follows the representative first pass.
- Emblem-like or unreadable logo marks are paired with the authoritative business name.
- Abstract process language such as `organized around`, `journey`, `path`, and `talk through` is rejected in favor of the concrete service, choice, or outcome.

The last two copy/identity rules landed after the A & T run. A To Z validated the readable-name rule; its one remaining `organized around` heading prompted the final direct-language correction. These are prompt/skill improvements, not deterministic content transforms.

## Google aggregate-rating evidence

The rating is a narrow structured observation, not ordinary authored copy. The display string is exact, individual third-party review prose is forbidden, and factual verification rejects a changed number.

A 10-business live-search screen produced 6 matches and 5 exact agreements with older retained observations. Higher search context reduced exact agreement; a more expensive model found fewer businesses and rounded one 4.9 rating to 5.0. Visible-browser inspection confirmed that several live-search omissions were extraction failures even though the Google panels were public and unambiguous.

The canonical evidence order is now:

1. An existing immutable rating observation attached to the build input.
2. A browser-observed prospect rating, only for an exact normalized first-party website match.
3. One bounded live web-search lookup with retained identity evidence.
4. Clean omission when no unambiguous exact observation exists.

This makes prospect previews reliable without a Google API or an automated Google-results-page scraper. It is intentionally a static build observation. A future approved, owner-connected Google integration is the correct boundary for continuous published-site refresh.

## Remaining limits

- Two sites establish credible quality and meaningful brand adaptation, not broad cross-vertical generality.
- Google live-search extraction alone is not reliable enough to be the only source for prospect previews.
- The current visual model and numeric Website Health diagnostic are not calibrated finely enough to rank close designs; frozen human production-readiness review remains authoritative.
- Source media quality varies. The author should continue to judge whether retained images improve the site rather than being forced to use them.

Complete the authority, source-preservation, image-evidence, and browser-gate corrections; run the clean release verification; then generate fresh, unambiguous businesses and inspect every route at desktop and phone sizes. Deployment and broader prebuild evaluation follow only after those fresh artifacts pass. One-off tastes remain owner-editable source decisions, but exact factual and functional failures block acceptance.
