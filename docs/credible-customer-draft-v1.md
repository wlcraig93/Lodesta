# Credible Customer Draft V1

**Status:** Historical rubric; deferred for reconsideration during pilot planning

**Version:** `credible-customer-draft-v1`

A generated site passes only when every item below is true. Reviewers record `pass` or `fail` with one concise observation for each item. Criteria do not change after outputs are generated.

1. **Distinct identity:** The site has a business-specific visual identity and does not look like a named template, a minor variant of another fixture, or generic AI output.
2. **Coherent experience:** Information hierarchy, navigation, page relationships, and the primary conversion path are clear on desktop and mobile.
3. **Grounded presentation:** Services, proof, business details, and marketing copy are supported by eligible canonical facts or evidence and suit the business.
4. **Finished execution:** There are no placeholders, broken media, obvious AI residue, incoherent overlaps, clipped text, unusable controls, or unfinished desktop/mobile states.
5. **Customer-ready:** The reviewer would send the draft to the business as a credible proposal without first manually redesigning it.

## Frozen Quality-Spike Edits

Apply one edit to each fixture without changing facts or unrelated pages:

- `austin-dent-paint-rich-media`: restyle the homepage hero to feel more editorial while retaining its content, actions, and media.
- `east-austin-collision-sparse`: strengthen the service hierarchy without inventing services, proof, or media.
- `north-loop-collision-no-media-proof`: revise the selected primary CTA presentation without changing its destination or surrounding facts.
- `south-congress-collision-service-breadth`: add a dedicated page for an existing eligible service and update navigation coherently.

Each edit passes only when the requested change is visible, objective gates remain green, no unrelated route or canonical fact changes, and the resulting site still passes all five criteria.
