# Independent review and ordinary editing

September 7, 2026. Completed private engineering experiment; no production reviewer stage or model-default change.

## Result

A fresh-context reviewer inspected all 29 route implementations and opened 42 rendered screenshots, including at least one view of every route, for the strongest retained Good Morning Tree Service candidate. It found no high-confidence material delivery defect within that coverage, and one minor actionable issue: visitors could not tell which contact fields were required. This is not an assertion that every route, state, claim or accessibility requirement was exhaustively tested.

The exact form definition confirmed that Name and Email are required and Phone and Message are optional. An ordinary private owner-edit run added that explanation immediately before the fields. It passed the hard gate without failed tools or transport recovery. The independent reviewer accepted the fix after opening six new Contact screenshots across desktop, tablet and phone.

The final comparison verifies:

- Exactly one paragraph added to `pages.tsx`; the other ten source files are byte-identical. No form schema, labels, destination, route metadata or public-input authority changed.
- All 29 routes captured at three widths: 87 observations, zero capture errors or page overflow.
- 171 of 179 screenshot PNGs are byte-identical. All eight changed images belong to Contact; no other route changed visually in those captures.
- The original version remains retained. The new candidate remains private and owned. No prospect was published, inquiry sent or notification enabled.

TypeScript and browser-render verification passed. Source/pixel comparison and a gate pass do not substitute for actual lead delivery; the separately completed Lodesta-owned canary supplies that workflow evidence, not a submission to this business.

## Decision

Keep the current authoring and editing architecture. The experiment demonstrates that evidence-backed independent feedback can be applied precisely through existing tools. It does not show that a permanent second agent is necessary or cost-effective for every build, especially because this was an already-refined candidate. Do not add automatic critics, repair continuations, another queue or a subjective release gate.

The design-review and copy-editing skills guided source-backed, route-specific inspection and preservation of working content. Their generic marketing suggestions did not authorize invented guarantees, altered quotations or unsupported professional claims.

## Remaining website delivery work

1. **Technology disclosures:** the retained cookie/privacy documents describe old-site technologies. The deployed minimal-analytics implementation does not make that inherited text accurate. Complete explicit owner-authorized document replacement through the existing immutable authority path; do not weaken legal preservation globally or silently rewrite original evidence. This is a delivery boundary, not another aesthetic revision.
2. **Qualification evidence:** the original About page names a professional credential, but the exact public input has no corresponding approved proof. Do not describe this as the author ignoring usable credential authority, and do not invent current certification or employment. Resolve through source/owner evidence where appropriate; the omission alone is not a universal release blocker.
3. **Unchanged configuration screen:** the canonical model remains Luna; the strongest private candidate uses Astra and subsequent edits. Choose and freeze the candidate configuration, then execute the declared eight-build/six-business screen with held-out cases. It has not started. This successful refinement does not count as a fresh pass in that screen.

The retained 4.7 rating and Google reviews link match their retained browser evidence. This experiment did not refresh that observation and must not be described as live Google synchronization.
